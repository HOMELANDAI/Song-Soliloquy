# Analysis Workflow

## Phase 1 — Ingest

Capture song metadata and section the supplied lyrics without rewriting the lyric text.

## Phase 2 — Research

Collect relevant production/music facts and cultural references. Track where claims came from and distinguish verified facts from interpretation.

## Phase 3 — Macro read

Establish mood, major themes, narrator perspective, dramatic tension, and the song's central thesis.

## Phase 4 — Bar analysis

Select important bars/sections and produce BAR / BREAKDOWN / DEEPER READ / TECHNICAL NOTES / WHY IT HITS.

## Phase 5 — Whole-lyrics annotation sweep

Independently sweep every line for metaphors, similes, symbols, personification, imagery, and credible double/triple entendres. Preserve exact anchor strings.

## Phase 6 — Editorial selections

Choose 5–10 key bars and exactly one hottest bar. Score the hottest bar 1–100.

## Phase 7 — Creative transformation

Build raw notes, NotebookLM source pack/questions, YouTube-ready angle, visual/B-roll ideas, SEO, and related-song comparisons.

## Phase 8 — Canonical export

Populate the `song-soliloquy/v1.1` JSON. The readable analysis and JSON must agree.

## Phase 9 — Validate

Run structural, anchoring, completeness, slug, SEO-length, and bar-presence checks. Do not mark the export complete until they pass.

## Phase 10 — Publish and connect

Import into the website and attach defensible related-song relationships so each analysis strengthens the wider catalog.
