# YouTube / Video Workflow

## Episode architecture

1. **Cold open** — strongest surprising claim about the song.
2. **Context** — artist, project, producer, release context, mood and thesis.
3. **Production DNA** — BPM, harmony/key/chords where supported, sample lineage, sonic texture, structure.
4. **Lyrical architecture** — rhyme patterns, cadence, recurring devices, narrator voice.
5. **Bar laboratory** — selected bars with layered interpretation and technical analysis.
6. **Reference decoder** — cultural, historical, street, sports, film, music, and other references.
7. **The deeper thesis** — what the record ultimately says about its subject/worldview.
8. **Connections** — comparable songs by musical or lyrical DNA.
9. **Hottest Bar** — reveal, explanation, heat score.
10. **Closing thesis / CTA** — memorable synthesis plus audience question.

## Signature Connections segment

Connections are a core differentiator. Avoid generic “sounds like” recommendations. State the dimension of similarity: BPM pocket, chord progression, sample family, rhyme construction, reference, wordplay mechanism, theme, or narrative stance.

## Visual system

Potential recurring visual modules:

- lyric phrase highlight;
- rhyme-color overlays;
- BPM/key cards;
- chord/progression diagrams;
- sample lineage timeline;
- reference cards;
- key-bar cards;
- heat-score meter;
- related-song web;
- B-roll prompts from raw notes.

## Source relationship

The video script is a derivative publishing artifact. It may simplify and sequence the analysis for retention, but it should not silently change the canonical interpretation or invent unsupported research facts.
